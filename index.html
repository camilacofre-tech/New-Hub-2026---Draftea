Empresa
Nuestro Propósito

Construir la plataforma de entretenimiento deportivo más emocionante de Latinoamérica — donde los fanáticos puedan predecir, competir y ganar.

Misión

Combinamos tecnología de punta, datos en tiempo real y una experiencia de usuario excepcional para transformar cómo las personas viven el deporte.

Visión 2026

Ser la plataforma líder de sports betting y fantasy sports en LATAM, con presencia en 10+ países y más de 1 millón de usuarios activos. Con Rei do Pitaco somos la plataforma de fantasy más grande de Brasil, con más de 10 millones de usuarios.

Empresa
Nuestros Valores

Who we are, permanently.

🔥 Passion

Our industry is about passion: for sports, for games, for moments that make people scream with joy. We need passionate people to build what comes next.

Looks like
Bringing energy to work Caring deeply about outcomes Fighting for what makes the product better
Ask yourself: What am I passionate for?

🎯 Ownership & Empowerment

We're owners, not employees. We see what needs to happen and we build it. We don't wait. We're persistent and brave in the face of challenges. We make decisions with conviction and move forward without fear.

Looks like
Taking responsibility for outcomes Fixing problems you see Making confident decisions Empowering others Moving forward despite uncertainty
Ask yourself: Am I acting like an owner or just completing tasks?

📊 Measured Impact

We love measuring impact and HAVING impact. Data-driven to our core. We ship things that move the needle. We demand quality in everything we do: product, processes, culture, and impact.

Looks like
Defining success metrics upfront Tracking what matters Making data-driven decisions Learning from results Delivering high-quality work Caring about details
Ask yourself: Can I prove my work is having impact?

❤️ Customer Centricity

We put customers at the center. We build WITH them, not just FOR them. Every decision starts with: "Does this help our customers?"

Looks like
Starting decisions with customer impact Listening and acting on feedback Obsessing over their problems Protecting customer experience
Ask yourself: Would I love this if I were the customer?

🤝 Collaboration

Across areas, products and countries, we're one company helping each other. No silos. The best ideas come from working together. We treat users, partners, and teammates with respect. We speak the truth and follow rules and processes.

Looks like
Sharing knowledge generously Helping whoever needs it Building bridges Celebrating team wins and losses
Ask yourself: Am I building bridges or walls?
